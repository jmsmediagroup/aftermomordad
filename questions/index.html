After Mom or Dad

Every question in the books

Each chapter answers one question. Find yours: each page has the short answer, what to do, what can wait, and who can help.

Book 1: The First Month

The first month after the death. Who to call, what to find, what can wait.

  1. What do I do in the first hour?
  2. Who do I call, and in what order?
  3. What if the death was sudden, or far from home?
  4. How many death certificates do I need, and how do I get them?
  5. Who is in charge if no one was named?
  6. How do I find the will and the papers, and what if I can't?
  7. What do I do about the house, pets, car and mail right now?
  8. How do I pay for the funeral?
  9. What if my sibling and I disagree about the funeral?
  10. Who tells Social Security and the other offices?
  11. Who is trying to scam me?
  12. What do I tell my employer, and what about me?
  13. What can wait until after the funeral?

Book 2: Being in Charge

Months 1 to 9 after the death. Executor or administrator duties, with or without a will.

  1. Am I the executor, and do I have to accept?
  2. What is probate, and does this estate need it?
  3. What if there is no will?
  4. What if my parent lived in another state?
  5. How do I get the legal authority to act?
  6. Do I need a lawyer?
  7. What do I tell creditors, and am I responsible for the debts?
  8. How do I find all of their accounts?
  9. What goes in the estate bank account?
  10. How long will this take?
  11. Can I be paid for this?
  12. When can I hand out the inheritance?
  13. How do I protect myself personally?

Book 3: Their Money

Months 1 to 12 after the death. Benefits, accounts, taxes.

  1. What happens with Social Security payments after the death?
  2. What happens to their pension or annuity?
  3. How do I claim life insurance?
  4. What happens to a 401(k) or IRA, and who pays tax on it?
  5. Who files the final tax return, and when?
  6. Can Medicaid recover costs from the estate or the house?
  7. What happens to their bank and investment accounts?
  8. Do I owe tax on what I inherit?
  9. How do I stop identity theft on a dead person's accounts?
  10. What about crypto, online accounts and digital assets?

Book 4: Their House

Months 3 to 15 after the death. Stay or sell, and clearing a life.

  1. Do I have to decide about the house now?
  2. Keep, sell or rent?
  3. Who pays taxes, insurance and utilities in the meantime, and what changes when a home is vacant?
  4. What happens to a mortgage or reverse mortgage?
  5. What is the tax on selling an inherited house?
  6. Who can sell the house, and what does it take?
  7. How do I clear a house in stages?
  8. What is worth selling, and what is an estate sale?
  9. What do I do with photos and papers I cannot throw away?
  10. What about medications, firearms, vehicles and devices?

Book 5: Siblings and the Estate

Months 1 to 18 after the death. Family, fairness, and who gets what.

  1. Why are we fighting when we always got along?
  2. One of us is in charge: what do the others have a right to know?
  3. What if the will is unequal?
  4. What if one of us cared for Mom or Dad for years?
  5. What if we cannot agree about the house?
  6. What if I think a sibling took money?
  7. How do we divide belongings without a war?
  8. What about half-siblings and step-siblings?
  9. When is a mediator worth it?
  10. What if I am estranged from a parent or sibling?
  11. How do I stay in my family afterward?

Book 6: Your Surviving Parent

Months 3 to 24 after the death. When the other parent is now alone.

  1. Is my other parent safe to live alone?
  2. What changes for their money, taxes and benefits?
  3. Who has power of attorney now, and does it need updating?
  4. Should we talk about their will now?
  5. What if the loss has revealed memory problems?
  6. Stay, downsize or move?
  7. How do I help without taking over my own life?
  8. Why is my parent's grief different from mine?
  9. Which scams target newly widowed people?
  10. Where should my mother or father start?

Book 7: The Second Parent

After the second death after the death. When the other parent dies too: the last estate, and the family after.

  1. What is different the second time?
  2. What changes now that both parents are gone?
  3. Who is in charge this time?
  4. What if the first estate was never finished?
  5. What happens to the house now?
  6. How do we divide everything with no parent left to decide?
  7. What if there is a stepparent or a new partner?
  8. Why are my siblings and I drifting apart?
  9. What is it like to have no parents left?
  10. What do we do with the family's history?

If today is very bad: call or text 988, or chat at chat.988lifeline.org, any hour. It is for anyone who is struggling; you do not have to be suicidal to reach out.

Veterans Crisis Line, also for family and friends: dial 988, then press 1, or text 838255. Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741. In an emergency, call 911.
